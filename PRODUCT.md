# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students and study-group managers who organize course documents, collaborate in subject workspaces, and use AI against authorized learning material from a phone.

## Product Purpose

AI Study Hub Mobile provides a focused document workspace for uploading, sharing, reviewing, and discussing study material while preserving subject, team, and per-document access rules from the web application.

## Brand Personality

Clear, dependable, and focused.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative glass effects, gradients, oversized rounded cards, desktop layouts compressed onto a phone, and permission controls whose state is not explicit.

## Design Principles

- Put documents first and keep workspace context visible.
- Make effective access and team membership understandable at a glance.
- Use familiar native mobile interactions for selection, sheets, and destructive actions.
- Show actionable loading, empty, success, pending, and failure states.
- Enforce permissions on the server and mirror them honestly in the interface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast in light and dark themes, minimum 44-point touch targets, text labels alongside important icons, screen-reader labels, and state changes that do not rely on color alone.
